CubeLab does not operate a server that can recover data stored only in your browser.

You are responsible for keeping backups of important data. Use CubeLab’s native export feature, and if you use Google Drive, verify that synchronization is working and that you understand its retention and conflict behavior. A backup is only useful if you can restore it; test restores when practical.

Large native backups may be compressed with gzip for size. Gzip is not encryption. Backup files may contain your complete solve and Training history and should be stored securely.

Google Drive synchronization is an eventual merge, not a guaranteed backup or a transactional mirror. See the Privacy Policy and the in-app Drive dialog for details.

The other participant and network infrastructure may receive technical connection information, such as IP addresses, as part of establishing the connection. Only battle with people you trust. Do not use Online Battle for sensitive information. Room IDs and passwords are access gates, not a guarantee of the other person’s identity or conduct.
